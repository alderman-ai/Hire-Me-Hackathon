Outreach doesn't have to pick between personalization and volume anymore. **Now you can do both. And this is not known yet.**

That gap is a knowledge arbitrage. Usually when something is very valuable, it's because you know something before other people do. This one is the reason this whole site exists. I said on camera I'd write a full article on it, so here it is.

## You've been on the receiving end of this

Especially if you've been running RevOps (revenue operations) or a marketing department. When you reached out to people before AI, there were two things you had to balance: personalization and volume.

You could be highly personalized. You could do heavy research, but that takes a lot of time, so you'd be very limited to your key accounts. Or volume: you could just shotgun blast lots of people.

> [!quote]
> Most people ended up in the middle or the shotgun blast. And at the time, shotgun blast was actually correct.
> Alex Alderman, on camera during the hackathon, 2 Nov 2026

And people intuitively knew. They knew when they were being shotgun blasted, and they knew when you were taking the time for it to be personalized. That was a very costly signal.

## What doing both looks like

Inbound marketing is great, and it has its own interaction with AI (AEO, GEO, whatever: getting found by AI search). But nothing compares to what's now possible with outbound marketing and AI. And I'm going to tell you why.

So for 12 hours I built in public for one company, Addvery. Think of it as max personalization for outreach.

**The personalization.** I research the whole org chart deeply, with the help of lots of tools to scrape information. (Just to be open, because your people might want to do that too.) I build a profile of all the key decision makers. If anyone's been there less than six months, I want to see the job advert they replied to, in the Wayback Machine or by scrubbing all the portals, because a lot of roles are fuzzy when it comes to responsibilities. Sometimes I get your 90-day goals too. If you're in month four and I have your 90-day goals from your job advert, that's gold for crafting custom messaging. (You should be doing this to your own targets too. You should see how it looks.)

**The volume.** 36 workflows in GetSales, the outreach tool: four main ICPs (ideal customer profiles), English, Czech and German, and three tactics. Every workflow's first message is a special variable, calculated before the lead CSV gets imported, and the walkthrough shows how a classifier would add each lead to the right one. It needs to be legible more than it needs to be functional: nothing sends, and no real data ran through it.

And instead of a page for different services, every person I feel might be involved in hiring me gets a page of their own, with a customized pitch.

## Why it's an arbitrage

Right now this is legitimately a costly signal. I'm spending all day on it, probably this evening and maybe some of tomorrow morning, and it's on camera, so you know it's costly. I cannot do this to 30 companies a day yet. I had to build it for the first time, and I wouldn't have been able to if I hadn't been working on this stuff for six months.

But I'm building it now on camera, and once it's built, it's built. With all the automations in place, I could do almost everything I built in those 12 hours through AI. Then it looks costly, and it isn't.

Everyone's blasting out AI right now, low quality, high volume. Against that, this stands out.

> [!quote]
> So good that they think you spent three days building it just for them when an agent was running in the background for a couple hours.
> Alex Alderman, on camera during the hackathon, 2 Nov 2026

**That's the knowledge arbitrage: people do not know that it's possible to give this level of customization through automations.** Whoever knows first gets the costly signal without the cost.

## Your top 100 accounts, then your whole CRM

It took me 12 hours, but here's the thing: all of the skills we built are reusable. I could do the same thing tomorrow for a new company, and the time would be cut in half. So would the stress.

Now imagine your top 100 accounts, if you sent them something with as much outreach effort as I'm putting in here. Would that be valuable? Maybe next month, after we test everything, right? It's not free.

Then keep going. What if every single person in your CRM could have this level of personalization?

> [!quote]
> Imagine your conversion rate if this is how you reached out to everyone in your CRM. Not only is it possible but the ROI would be unbelievable.
