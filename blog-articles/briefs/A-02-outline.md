## The strongest objections

1. **Revenue-side AI cuts jobs too.** Pipeline AI does work that SDRs and researchers did. More revenue with fewer people is still fewer people.
   Question: whose work does it take over, and where do those people go?
2. **The gains get pocketed, not shared.** A company can grow revenue with AI and cut staff anyway.
   Question: what makes a company spend the new revenue on its team?
3. **A GTM engineer selling GTM engineers.** The author is applying for this job, and the argument ends where he gets paid.
   Question: what would you still argue if you weren't applying?
