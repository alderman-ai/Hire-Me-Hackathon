## Counterarguments to answer

1. **Sold as an SDR-team replacement.** A vendor headline: "How GTM Engineering Is Replacing SDR Teams" (Factors.ai, 24 Jun 2026; vendor). And 36% of 560+ venture-backed B2B software firms cut SDR headcount in the year to April 2025 (Emergence Capital via SaaStr; investor data). Both `verified 2 Nov 2026`. *Answer:* prompt 6.
2. **More business, same people.** Salesforce cut support from 9,000 to about 5,000, redeploying "hundreds of employees" into sales and elsewhere (The Register, 2 Sep 2025). `verified 2 Nov 2026` *Answer:* is "some teams grow" enough?
3. **Returns so far are in cost.** Over half of GenAI budgets go to sales and marketing, but "the biggest ROI" is "in back-office automation" (MIT NANDA via Fortune, 18 Aug 2025; preliminary). `verified 2 Nov 2026` *Answer:* raise it and give your reading?
4. **The gains get pocketed.** Challenger: naming AI in a layoff "can win over investors while pushing current and prospective employees away" (6 Aug 2026). `verified 2 Nov 2026` *Answer:* prompt 9; it stays the reader's call.
5. **Self-interest.** Named in the lede. *Answer:* prompt 10.
