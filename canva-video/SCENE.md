## What this is

The site you're building today is a personalised job application to Addvery, made live during a recorded 12-hour hackathon. One part of it is a short video of you designing in Canva. You build one Addvery LinkedIn graphic yourself, from an asset pack made out of your pixel-perfect branded assets work, following one of its mockups, then export it. It shows Addvery's marketing side that you can do the design work yourself, not only automate it, and that the pack is something a person can build from straight away. The video goes on YouTube, unlisted, and gets its own section on the site.

The asset pack is ready, so you can film the scene **whenever it suits you tonight**, in your normal recorded rig session (type `/start` first if you're between sessions). It's about 20 minutes in all. That's about 5 minutes of setup with the recording paused, then 10 to 12 minutes on camera, never more than 15, because YouTube caps unverified channels at 15 minutes. The video shows your screen, with your webcam in a small corner. Once you've said the closing line, nothing more is needed from you. The clip waits until you `/stop` and `/process` that session, as usual. Then it's cut, checked frame by frame for anything private, and put on the site.

What's waiting on it: the video's section on the site. No deadline beyond tonight.

## Step 1. Get the screen ready, with the recording paused

About 5 minutes. Type `/pause` first (or do all of this before `/start`).

1. **Notifications off.** `Win+N`, then Do not disturb. A pop-up from email or chat can carry a client's name.
2. **Clear the left monitor.** Move everything except one Chrome window to the 4K monitor: terminals, Obsidian, this Doc, email, and any Chrome window the assistants are working in.
3. **One clean Chrome window for the scene,** with no other tabs open.
4. **Clear Chrome's download list:** open `chrome://downloads` and click Clear all. The files stay on disk. After you export, Chrome pops up a list of recent downloads, and older ones could show a client's file name.
5. **Downloads save without asking:** in Chrome, Settings, then Downloads, check that "Ask where to save each file before downloading" is off. If it's on, a save dialog showing a folder's files appears on camera when you export.
6. **Your own Canva account,** not a client's team. If you belong to more than one Canva team, switch to your own first.
7. **Make the empty design:** from Canva's home, Create a design, then Custom size, <width × height> px. Name it <design name>. This is the only time Canva's home is on screen, and the recording is paused.
8. **Stage the pack's parts on a second page.** Add a page, then drag the pack's images from an Explorer window on the 4K monitor onto page 2. That page is your parts tray: on camera you build page 1 from it, so the Uploads panel never needs to open. If Canva opens a panel while it uploads, close it. Go back to page 1.
9. **Check the brand sections once.** Click any text box, then open the colour picker, the font list and the Text tab on the left. Each can show a brand kit by name, with its colours and fonts. If any shows another client's kit, switch the selector to Addvery's, or plan not to open that panel on camera. Close them again.
10. **Find Download:** check that the File menu at the top left has Download. If it does, you can export without the Share panel, which lists who has access. Open it once and pick the export settings from Step 2 item 8. If Canva shows an upgrade prompt, your plan doesn't include them: keep the defaults on camera. Close it without downloading.
11. **Close the left side panel** if one is open, so the scene starts on the empty page.
12. **On the 4K monitor:** open the mockup you're following (<mockup file>), the pack's sheet of fonts, colours and sizes (<sheet file>), and the copy (<where the copy is>). Copy and paste the copy from there. Don't open Windows' clipboard history (`Win+V`): it shows past clips.
13. **Full screen:** press `F11` in Chrome. It hides the tabs, the address bar with the design's link, the bookmarks bar and the extension icons.
14. Type `/pause` again to resume the recording.

## Step 2. Design on camera

1. Say **"Canva design starts now"**.
2. Say what you're about to make, in one sentence of your own, before you start. The first few seconds carry most of a video's viewing, so the outcome goes first. After that, talk as you go only if it comes naturally.
3. **Build page 1 from the parts on page 2.** Copy or drag each part across, add the text boxes, set the fonts and colours from the pack's sheet, then size and align everything to the mockup. Lock the background and logo once they're placed, if you like.
4. **Stay out of these on camera.** They list things from across your whole account:
   - the **Projects**, **Uploads** and **Brand** tabs in the left panel;
   - Canva's home page (the Canva logo or the back arrow at the top left);
   - the **Share** panel (it lists who has access);
   - your profile menu at the top right (it lists your teams);
   - any file picker or Explorer window on this monitor.
5. **Need something that isn't on page 2?** Type `/pause`, add it, then type `/pause` again. Paused time never reaches the video. Don't crop an image to hide part of it: in a PDF export, the cropped-out part can be recovered by anyone who downloads it.
6. **No real person** in the design: no name and no photo of anyone. The copy is chosen so it names no one.
7. **Delete page 2** when page 1 is done, so the export holds only the finished graphic.
8. **Export** through the File menu: Download, then <PNG, quality High, size 1x | for a carousel: PDF Standard, with Flatten PDF on>. If Canva shows an upgrade prompt when you change the quality, keep the defaults. Don't open the downloaded file on camera. Finish on the finished graphic in Canva, zoomed to fit.
9. Say **"Canva design done"**.

**If something goes wrong**
- **Something private shows for a moment:** carry on. Write down roughly when, in Step 4. The check before upload blurs or cuts it.
- **A secret shows (a key, a password):** type `/pause` at once and say so in Step 4.
- **You want a retake:** say "Canva design starts now" again and start over. The cut runs from the last start line to "Canva design done".
- **Running long:** past 12 minutes, wrap up. The cut trims waiting and exporting, and never changes the order.

## Step 3. After the scene

1. Press `F11` to leave full screen. Turn Do not disturb off if you like.
2. Carry on with the rig session as usual. The clip waits until you `/stop` and `/process` it. Nothing about that changes.

## Step 4. Report

Leave a comment on this Doc: roughly when you said each line, and anything that went wrong or showed on screen that shouldn't have. "All fine" is enough.

## When you are done

Your comment on this Doc is all it takes. The assistant waits for the session to be filed, asks for the clip between your two lines, cuts it, has a separate check look at a frame at every scene change and at least every 5 seconds, then hands the video to the site.
