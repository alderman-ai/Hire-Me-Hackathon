In this video I design one Addvery LinkedIn graphic in Canva by hand: a 1080 × 1080 quote card, started from a blank page. My AI agents made the asset pack I build it from, with my pixel-perfect branded-assets method.

It shows Addvery's marketing team two things. I can do the design work myself, not only automate it. And the pack is something a person can build from straight away.

## What I'm building

A quote on a dark background, with a gradient quote mark, a name and role, a thin rule and Addvery's white logo. The quote comes from one of the [LinkedIn posts in Addvery's voice](/demos/linkedin-posts-in-addverys-voice-graded-blind), so the graphic and the posts read as one campaign.

## The asset pack

The pack covers five Addvery LinkedIn templates, from a stat card to a six-page carousel. Each comes as a finished mockup first, the way I asked for it:

> [!quote]
> You're going to make a bunch of mockups of something that could be used as a template right out the gate.
> Alex Alderman, in a message to his AI agents, 2 Nov 2026

Behind each mockup sit the brand art as PNGs, build sheets with every element's values in build order, and the copy, ready to paste. On camera I bring the quote mark and logo across from a second page and place each one from the sheet.

## Pixel-perfect in Canva

Five things get the Canva build onto the render's pixels.

**Brand art locked, text left as text.** The quote mark and logo go in as PNGs and, with the rule, are fully locked. The quote, name and role are native Canva text, locked in position only, so the words stay editable.

**Every element measured.** The templates share one grid: 96 px side margins, 12 columns, an 8 px baseline. The sheet gives every element's X, Y, W and H.

**Inter, tuned.** Of the brand's faces, only Inter is in Canva's library, so every text box is set in Inter with its own letter-spacing value. Canva's font-size box is in points, not px, so every size had to be scaled by 0.75: the quote's 56 px became 42.

**A gradient on two words.** The brand gradient sits on "real commitment". If Canva won't colour two words inside a text box, the sheet's second route is a copy of the box holding only those words, laid exactly over the original.

**A 50% overlay check.** The render goes over the build at 50% transparency. The mark, rule and logo should match exactly, and every quote line within 1 px. Only the name and role letters differ: Canva lacks the face the render uses.

## Why I build it by hand

The first plan was to film me customising a finished template. I asked for the parts instead: "Give me all the assets I need to build it myself".

A pack only proves itself when someone builds from it. The finished card is a template too: the brand art is locked and the text is free, so the next quote is a paste, not a rebuild.
