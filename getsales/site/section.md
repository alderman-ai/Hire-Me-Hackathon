## Guardrails

Sending to real people is where outbound goes wrong, so the guardrails are part of the build:

- **Nothing sends without a person's yes.** Every message to the six was approved by Alex, word for word, before the flow started.
- **Only the live run sends.** The demo workflows have no leads and were never started.
- **A notice at the first contact.** Every message links to a short privacy notice: who, why, what's kept, until when, and how to object.
- **One word stops it.** Any reply stops the sequence; "no" deletes what's held and nothing more is sent.
- **Human pace.** Sends are spread out within the sender's own limits, never a burst.
- **Minimal data.** Name, role, company, profile URL and one opening line. Deleted by 31 December 2026 unless a conversation continues.
- **Nothing about them on the page.** The six appear here only as roles; names, photos and replies stay private.
