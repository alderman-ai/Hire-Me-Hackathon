## What's being approved

### For the GetSales outreach demo

This builds your model: Addvery's ICPs × the languages that make sense × three approaches, as workflows in one new GetSales folder. Each workflow starts from the first message imported as a lead field. The six-person alert also goes through it. Two earlier questions, about Sales Navigator export and a ten-ICP classifier, are dropped, because your decision on the scraping demo replaced them.

**Question 1. How GetSales works for this build (read-only check of its guides).** What can GetSales do for this build: workflows grouped in a folder, importing leads with a custom field such as `custom message 1` and using it as the opening message, custom variables in message templates, and sending different leads to different workflows? What are the limits: per-sender daily caps, LinkedIn safety limits, how an import maps columns to fields, and what a workflow with no leads and not started looks like?

**Question 2. The computed first message.** What does good LLM-computed personalisation in a first LinkedIn message look like in 2026 (signal-based openers, one researched line), how should it differ across three approaches and across languages, what evidence is there on reply rates, and what reads as AI-written?

**Plus three wildcards and a summary** for the GetSales demo.

### For the website

The site is at once a live build log, ten demos and six stakeholder sections, in Addvery's branding. Your own brand capture isn't researched here. This is only about the mechanics.

**Question 3. Structure and how each visitor finds their part.** For a site that is at once a live build log, ten demos and six stakeholder sections, what structure lets a named visitor find "their" part in seconds: one long page with anchors, a page per stakeholder or role (for example `/for/cto`), or a personalised landing route? What do 1:1 account-based-marketing microsites converge on, and what are the traps (search indexing of named pages, links forwarded inside the company, mobile reading)?

**Question 4. What a stakeholder section contains.** What do 1:1 ABM pages and buying-committee personalisation put in a section for one role (CEO, CTO, CSO, RevOps, a chief AI officer, a co-founder) so a senior reader engages: the problem framed for that role, one proof, one small ask? What evidence is there on what makes them act, and what reads as generic?

**Question 5. The brand pass.** What's the fastest reliable way to apply another company's look to a Next.js site from its public website (colour and type tokens, spacing, components), and what are the traps: font licensing, logo and trademark use without permission, the "unofficial" line, and a copy that reads as impersonation?

**Plus three wildcards and a summary** for the website.
