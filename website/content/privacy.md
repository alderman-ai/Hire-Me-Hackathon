
# Privacy notice

This is an unofficial job application by Alex Alderman. It is not affiliated with Addvery.

If you got a LinkedIn message from me about this build, this page explains why, what I hold about you, and how to make me stop.

## Who is responsible

Alex Alderman, an individual job applicant based in Prague. To reach me about your data, reply to my LinkedIn message.

## Why I contacted you

To apply for a role at Addvery and ask for a conversation about it. It's a job application, not a sales pitch.

## Legal basis

Legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)): my interest in applying for work. Before sending anything, I weighed it against your interests in a short written test.

## What I hold

Your name, role and employer, your public LinkedIn profile URL, public professional information, and one personalised opening line for my message. No email address, phone number or photo, and nothing from connections-only content.

## Where it came from

Public LinkedIn profiles, Addvery's public website, and web scraping of public pages with Apify. I did the research myself.

## Who processes it for me

- GetSales sends the LinkedIn messages.
- Anthropic's Claude helped me draft the opening lines.
- Apify ran the web scraping.
- Vercel hosts this site.

Some of these providers are based in the US, so your data may be processed outside the EU under their standard terms.

Anything you reply stays private: it won't appear on this site, in the recording or in the public code.

## How long I keep it

I delete it by 31 December 2026, unless we're still in a conversation then.

## Your right to object

You can object at any time, without giving a reason. Reply "no" or "stop" to my LinkedIn message and I will delete what I hold about you and stop contacting you, keeping only a note not to contact you again.

## Your other rights

You can ask to see what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or limit how I use it. Reply to my LinkedIn message and I'll act on it within a month.

## Complaints

You can complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ) at [uoou.gov.cz](https://uoou.gov.cz).
