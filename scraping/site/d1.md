<!-- (b) How it works -->

<!-- block: list -->
1. **I decided what to find out:** how Addvery wins work and what it's betting on next. A first message only works when it's about you.
2. **Public sources were read:** AI agents read the website, register and press. On Apify, a cloud scraping service, 6 scrapers read YouTube interviews, the company podcast and public LinkedIn pages, and a classification model sorted the posts and profiles. LinkedIn shows up here only as counts, like the ones on the boards, with no one named.
3. **It feeds the next demo:** the [outreach demo](/d3), built in GetSales, uses it to pick whom to write to and to write each personal first line.

<!-- block: p -->
Addvery's website, its employer profile, the business register and the press were read directly by agents and a web search before any actor ran, with no scraper, so they aren't in this table. Only the rows below ran on Apify, which calls its ready-made scrapers "actors".

<!-- block: table -->
| What it read | Apify actor (linked to its public Store page) | Results | Time | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Public LinkedIn pages of Addvery's leadership, its team and its company page (profiles and recent posts), used for the company-level counts on the boards and to choose whom to write to | [LinkedIn Profile Scraper](https://apify.com/supreme_coder/linkedin-profile-scraper), [LinkedIn Company Employees](https://apify.com/harvestapi/linkedin-company-employees), [LinkedIn Profile Posts](https://apify.com/harvestapi/linkedin-profile-posts) | 273 | 5.8 min | $1.06 |
| YouTube transcripts of interviews and the company podcast (first choice) | [YouTube Transcript Scraper](https://apify.com/supreme_coder/youtube-transcript-scraper) | none (blocked; see what broke, below) | 0.5 min | $0.12 |
| The same YouTube transcripts, second try | [YouTube Transcript Scraper](https://apify.com/pintostudio/youtube-transcript-scraper) (fallback) | 6 transcripts, about 4.3 hours of video | 1 min | $0.05 |
| A 74-minute talk with no captions, sent to AI transcription | [YouTube Transcript Scraper Pro](https://apify.com/codepoetry/youtube-transcript-ai-scraper) | none (see what broke, below) | 9.6 min | $0.01 |

<!-- block: list -->
- **Totals:** 6 actors in 30 runs, 279 results, about 17 minutes, $1.24 of the $10 spending cap I set. Then Jev, TypeSafe's classification model, labelled 50 posts and 57 profiles in 107 calls, for $0.003. The figures are as Apify and TypeSafe reported them for the runs on 2 Nov 2026.
- **Cleaning:** each output was cut to the fields actually used and the raw files deleted, reposts were tagged so nobody is credited with someone else's post, one "employee" turned out to be an outside English teacher and was taken out (57 became 56), and LinkedIn claims were checked against the register and the press.
- **What broke, and how it was handled:** the free plan caps a run at 25 items, so the employee list took page-by-page runs, and one of three pages started in the same second was rejected and rerun alone. The first transcript actor ran out of its network allowance on every video, so the fallback did all the transcripts; one 74-minute talk had no captions, and AI transcription gave up.
