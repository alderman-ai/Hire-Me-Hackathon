# Clip index: day one

## What this is

Every moment from the eleven recordings of the Hire Me Hackathon (2 November 2026, sessions S01 to S11) that is worth a clip, in one place. Each moment has a class, a score, its Prague time and where it sits in the recording, so Alex can browse the day and the agents can cut from it. Three agents read the transcripts in parallel and one merged their notes; every clip in the current batch was checked word by word, and the screen ones frame by frame. What stays out, and why, is listed at the end.

## Key

- **Class.** *Demo*: something working, on screen or described. *Insight*: an idea or a rule, in his words. *Low point*: stress, a mistake, a bad call. *Turning point*: a decision that changes the plan. *Humour*: a funny or human moment.
- **Score.** 1 to 5: how much a follower would get from it. 5 is the day's best.
- **Status.** `cut`: already a clip, with its YouTube id. `to cut`: in the current batch; `to cut (held)` means it is cut but waits for Alex's OK. `held`: screen footage that waits for Alex's OK, because the site shows the webcam, not the screen. `indexed`: noted, not cut.
- **Time.** *Prague* is the wall clock when the moment starts. *Range* is minutes and seconds into that session's recording, rounded outward to whole seconds, or inward at a hard edge (where the next words must stay out). The exact seconds for each cut are kept with the cut list.
- **Use.** *feed*: the site's live feed. *article*: the day-one article. *D1 to D11*: the demo entries (D1 scraping, D2 company research, D3 GetSales outbound, D5 blog articles, D6 Canva templates, D7 LinkedIn content, D8 the website, D9 this feed and how its moments get picked).
- **Cam.** *webcam*: only Alex at his desk. *screen*: his screen recording.
