### 14:10 · Research gets its first lists

The research agent drafts five research lists: one for the whole project, and one each for scraping, the blog pipeline, outbound and the brand assets. - How do the strongest "job application as outreach" sites make the work clear to a non-technical decision maker in under a minute?
- What tips a personalised website built as a job application from impressive to creepy, and what makes the recipient act on it?

Once he approves, one agent per question researches it, all in parallel.
