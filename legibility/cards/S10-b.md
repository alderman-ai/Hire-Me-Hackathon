## Points

- There's no such thing as building an AI workflow as a product; it's always a managed service.
- Why: the models always change.
- Every company has to decide what level of AI skill it wants.
- Three options: just use workflows other people build, maintain them itself, or build them itself.
- Imagine a vertical sliding scale: training at the bottom, external building at the top; each company has to find where it sits.
- Either it gets trained to do it itself, or it brings in or hires someone external.
- Even then, it needs training alongside whatever someone else builds, to actually use it ("compensate" in the transcript; probably "complement", unclear).
- He calls this the general scope of article one and sets the session working on it.
