## Points

- Something is usually valuable because of knowledge arbitrage: you know something before other people.
- People who have run rev ops or marketing departments have been on the receiving end of this.
- Before AI, outreach balanced two things: personalization and volume.
- Personalization meant heavy research and a lot of time, so it was limited to key accounts.
- Volume meant shotgun-blasting lots of people; most people ended up in the middle or with the shotgun blast, which was correct at the time.
- Now you don't have to pick between them: you can do both, and this isn't known yet.
- People intuitively knew when they were being shotgun-blasted (the thought carries on into S02-d).
