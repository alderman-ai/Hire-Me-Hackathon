*Why the first hours of AI work feel like the lowest leverage, and why you do them anyway.*

**One of the biggest things to learn in AI is that it's always front-loaded.** It's almost always worth putting the time in at the beginning, because everything can run overnight. We don't need 24 hours of wall clock for the automated work to happen.

So the most important thing is the parent document, the one every agent works from. We've got to invest time now and make sure it's right, because everything else is based off it.

That's a really tough call to sit with when it's already noon and you've got nothing running in parallel. Just me, on the bottleneck. **It feels really bad, because right now the work feels really low leverage.**
