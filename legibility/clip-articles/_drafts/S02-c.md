# Now you can do both

*Personalization or volume: before AI, you had to pick. Now you can do both, and that is not known yet.*

**You don't have to pick between personalization and volume anymore. Now you can do both.** And this is not known yet.

If you've been running rev ops or a marketing department, you've been on the receiving end of the old trade-off. When you reached out to people before AI, you could be highly personalized and do heavy research, but that takes a lot of time, so you'd be very limited to your key accounts. Or you could just shotgun blast lots of people. Most people ended up in the middle or the shotgun blast, and at the time, the shotgun blast was actually correct.

That's the knowledge arbitrage. Something's usually valuable because you know it before other people, right? **And most people still think they have to pick.**
