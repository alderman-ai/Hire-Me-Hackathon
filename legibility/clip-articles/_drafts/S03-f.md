# An even tighter bottleneck: my focus

*Why every question my agents had for me went to one desk, one at a time.*

If my time is the bottleneck, then **an even tighter bottleneck is my focus.** That's one of the core rules I set for this build, right at the start.

So there's a separate session that's the one place I interact with all the others. If any of them has a question for me, it sends it there, to one desk: its own monitor, one question at a time. We did this on a different project and it worked really well, so I stole it back.

Why bother? Because otherwise I miss questions. Things that are blocked by me just sit in line, in a chat, in one of the 14 or so sessions I'd have running. **One desk means nothing's waiting on me in a window I'm not looking at.**

(By five that afternoon I was still drowning in requests. The desk just meant I was drowning in one place.)
