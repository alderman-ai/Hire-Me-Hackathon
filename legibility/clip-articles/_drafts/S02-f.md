# The first place you put AI is your pipeline

*AI only saves money if it cuts a cost. If you won't cut the team, start with the pipeline.*

If you really want to keep your team and do AI the ethical way (whatever that means), **the first place you need to put AI is your pipeline.**

A lot of companies are going to say: we want to bring AI in, but we're not going to fire half the team. Okay, if you say so. But AI only saves you money if it reduces some cost, and for most people, the cost it's going to reduce is labor.

So you upskill your people to get more done, and at the same time you bring in more work. Then you pair those. That's why I think boosting pipelines is the role that's going to matter, and it's the job I took nine months to learn, coming from a marketing background.

Otherwise you're going to have some very uncomfortable decisions. **Your comfort levels may vary.**
