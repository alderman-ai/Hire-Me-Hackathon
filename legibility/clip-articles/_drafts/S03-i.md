# The three tribes of AI

*Academics, former devs, and entrepreneurs who are mostly marketers. One of my spiciest takes.*

**One of my spiciest takes: if you're a skilled AI practitioner right now, you probably came from one of three tribes.**

The academics: machine learning people, computer scientists, the ones who saw AI first. A lot of them still see it as quite abstract and are the least hands-on with commercial AI, but in a company they really know the security part of it.

The former devs, who got into AI from coding. They're really good at the deterministic toolkit: reading and writing code. Turns out that's pretty useful for this stuff. A little less required than they or the academics would believe, but still more required than the marketers out there think.

And the entrepreneurs, who are mostly marketers. Whatever your skill is, you also have to learn marketing to get clients, so if marketing is your skill, you get a two for one. (Same as being a native English speaker: your local language is the global one. I try to stack every advantage I can.)

**That's my tribe.** Our weakness at the beginning? It takes a lot of crazy stuff like this to get hired.
