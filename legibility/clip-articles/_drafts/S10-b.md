# No AI workflow is a product

*Build, maintain or train: every company has to pick its spot on the slider.*

There's no such thing as building an AI workflow as a product. **It's always a managed service**, because the models always change. (Yes, that includes anything I'd build for you.)

So every company is going to have to decide what level of AI skill it wants. Do you just use workflows other people build? Maintain them yourselves? Or build them yourselves?

Imagine a sliding scale that's vertical. On the bottom is the training, and on top is the external building. Every company has to find out where on the slider it is: either your people get trained how to do it themselves, or you bring in someone external, or hire someone.

Even then, whatever someone else builds still needs the training to actually get used. **So you're not really picking a vendor or a hire. You're picking how much of the service lives inside your team.**
