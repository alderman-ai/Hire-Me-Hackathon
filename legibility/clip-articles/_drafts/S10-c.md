# GTM engineers are how AI keeps its promise

*The ROI question behind every "we won't cut jobs" AI plan, answered in under a minute.*

**GTM (go-to-market) engineers are the solution to the biggest social question of the AI era.**

A lot of companies are saying AI isn't a way for them to fire half their staff. That's fine. But for AI to save a company any money, there has to be some return on that investment. And if it's not lowering their labor costs, then it has to be increasing their revenue.

Which means the GTM engineers who focus the AI on the pipeline are the ones actually helping companies keep that promise: AI as a way to do more business and have the whole team grow together, instead of replacing half of it. **The only way to get the leverage of AI without dropping half your team is to also leverage the amount of new work you're getting.**
