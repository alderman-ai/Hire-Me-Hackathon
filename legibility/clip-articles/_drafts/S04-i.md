# Mistakes cascade, and they just snowball

*Why the setup is worth slowing down for, even when you could have 20 agents running.*

**If you've worked with AI long enough, it's so much more time-efficient to make sure the part at the beginning is done well**, because mistakes cascade. They just snowball.

The hard part is that if you've been in AI long enough to know the leverage of having lots of things running in parallel, it's hard to take the time to make sure everything's right first. I said this while I was itching to get agents going immediately, because I felt like a major bottleneck.

But time is no longer the bottleneck. You can have 20 agents running side by side, right? **The bottleneck is focus. It's judgment, discernment.** That's why I try to put all the stuff that needs me early on.

So my plan for the rest of the day: get all the tooling set up, spend the time on everything that needs decisions or clarity from me until I'm about to leave the computer, maybe an hour to check it all, and then just press play and let it all build.
