1. **R2 and R3 (with W1 and W2): which plan has element locking.**
   - *The reports:* R2 found no plan line on the lock help page, but the pricing page lists "Element locking" and "Brand Controls" under Business and "Granular element controls" under Enterprise. R2 couldn't reconcile them. R3, W1 and W2 read the help page as putting Lock and "Lock position only" in every design, with role-bound locks only in Brand Templates (Pro and up "with Brand Kit access"; regional pages vary).
   - *Ruling:* there are two features with one name.
     - The padlock (Lock, and "Lock position only") is in any design. Nothing found gates it by plan, so S-D6 can set the build sheet's L and P levels on whatever plan Alex has.
     - The binding, role-bound locks of Brand Templates and Brand Controls are a paid feature. The help pages say Pro and up; the pricing page says Business. Whether Pro gets them stays open.
   - *Why:* the help page is the specific source on the feature. The pricing page is a summary whose per-plan ticks R2 couldn't read.
   - *Probe (2 minutes, off camera):* on the test import from conflict 4, select an element and open the padlock: are both options there? Read the plan in account settings. On a paid plan, check whether the Share menu offers "Brand Template". Never open a design the session didn't create.
2. **R2 and W1: what the Canva connector can do.**
   - *The reports:*
     - R2 (canva.dev, about 46 tools): a broad inventory, including Brand Templates, autofill, folders and export. The only documented edits replace text and image fills, with no move, resize, restyle or lock. A help snippet says Claude's tools were folded into `read_design` and `edit_design`.

   - *Ruling:* take R2 for the inventory, because it's Canva's official page. Take W1 for the edit operations, because Canva's own skill is more specific, though it was read via a mirror. The working assumption: the connector can fill, format, move and export existing elements, but it can't create a template's structure or lock it.
   - *Why it doesn't change today's call:* both reports' decisive gaps coincide (no lock, no font upload, no local-file import). W1's clock rules the connector out for today anyway, because it needs a fork plus about 30 minutes.

     - Call one plan-revealing read (`list-brand-kits` or `get-export-formats`).
     - Read the upload tool's schema to see whether it takes a local file.
     - No writes. It's a job for the morning, unless Alex adds the connector today.
